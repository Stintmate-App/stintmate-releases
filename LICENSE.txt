3. Your data
Your sessions, laps, notes and settings stay in the work folder you choose, on this PC. Stintmate
sends nothing on its own, with two exceptions:
- once a day it reads its public releases page to look for a new version (you can turn this off
  in Settings);
- a problem report is sent to the Stintmate team's server only when you press Send, after you
  have seen and can untick every file in it; the attached files are deleted when the report is
  closed.

4. Your AI agent
Your race engineer runs in the AI agent you choose (for example Claude Code, opencode, Codex CLI or
Antigravity CLI). The agent is a separate program under its provider's terms, with your own
account, plan or credits. What you say to your engineer is processed by that provider, not by
Stintmate.

5. Games
Stintmate reads the games' official telemetry through connectors. It never writes to a game's
folders and never changes the game.

6. Installation
The installer puts the program in your user profile (no administrator rights). Uninstalling removes
the program and never your work folder, your racing data or the games.
